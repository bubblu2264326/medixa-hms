# Reception Module User Guide

Welcome to the HMS Reception Module. This guide provides step-by-step instructions for managing patient registration, vitals intake, and doctor queues.

## 1. Getting Started
- **URL**: [HMS Portal](/receptionist)
- **Login**: Use your designated Receptionist credentials provided by the Administrator.

## 2. Patient Registration
If a patient is visiting for the first time, they must be registered in the system.

1.  Navigate to the **Patient Registration** tab.
2.  Fill in the **Patient Name**, **Age**, and **Gender**.
3.  Enter the **CNIC / Identity No.** using the format: `XXXXX-XXXXXXX-X`.
4.  Enter the **Contact Number** (starts with `03`).
5.  Provide the **Permanent Address**.
6.  Click **Add Patient** to save the record.

> [!TIP]
> Use the **Arrow Keys** (Left/Right) to navigate between form fields quickly.

## 3. Recording Vitals (Biometric Intake)
Once a patient is registered, their vitals should be captured before they see a doctor.

1.  Select the patient from the **Queue**.
2.  Navigate to the **Biometric Intake** section.
3.  Enter the following metrics:
    -   **Blood Pressure** (e.g., 120/80)
    -   **Temperature** (e.g., 98.6 °F)
    -   **Heart Rate** (BPM)
    -   **Height** (cm) and **Weight** (kg)
    -   **Blood Group** (e.g., O+)
4.  Click **Save Biometric Data**.

## 4. Queue Management & Doctor Assignment
This is where you assign patients to specific doctors.

1.  Use the **Search bar** to find a patient by Name or ID.
2.  Select the **Visit Type**:
    -   **OPD**: Routine outpatient visit.
    -   **Emergency**: Immediate priority case.
3.  **Assign Doctor**: Select the available practitioner from the dropdown.
4.  **Chief Complaint**: Briefly enter the reason for the visit.
5.  Click **Add to Queue**.

## 5. Specialist Clinical Intake (Gynaecology)
For female patients visiting the Gynaecologist, additional clinical details must be recorded.

### A. Menstrual History
Record the following if applicable:
-   **Menarch Age**, **Cycle Length**, and **Bleeding Duration**.
-   **Regularity**: Mark as Regular or Irregular.
-   **History**: Contraception, Gynae Surgeries, and any relevant Medical Conditions.
-   **Menopause Status**: Pre- or Post-menopausal.

### B. Obstetric History & Para Details
For patients with past pregnancies:
1.  **Obstetric Summary**: Quick count of pregnancies, deliveries, and living children.
2.  **Para Records**: Record details for each pregnancy:
    -   Birth Year/Month.
    -   Delivery Type (Normal, C-Section, etc.).
    -   Birth Weight and Gestational Age.
    -   Complications and Clinical Notes.

## 6. Troubleshooting & Support
-   **Patient Not Found?**: Double-check the ID or try searching by name. If they are new, use the Registration tab.
-   **Wrong Doctor Assigned?**: You can find the patient in the active queue and update their assignment.
-   **Clinical Data Not Saving?**: Ensure all mandatory fields are filled correctly.
-   **System Issues**: Contact the IT Admin if the portal is slow or unresponsive.

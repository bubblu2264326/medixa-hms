# Doctor Portal: User Guide

The Doctor Portal is a clinical workspace optimized for consultation and prescription workflows. It is accessed via the `/doctor` route.

## 1. Quick Start: The Patient Queue
The **Queue Sidebar** on the left is the engine of the portal. It displays all patients who have been registered and vitals-checked at reception.

1.  **Select Patient**: Click on a patient card in the queue to load their clinical workspace.
2.  **Toggle View**: Use the collapse/expand icon to hide the queue and maximize the consultation area.
3.  **Active Visit ID**: Once selected, the visit number and patient details appear in the header for verification.

## 2. Clinical Workspace (Patient Tab)
This is the primary tab for reviewing intake and documenting findings.

### Identity & Intake
- **Identity**: Check the patient's CNIC, contact, and address.
- **Active Visit**: Review the **Presenting Complaint** (why the patient is here today) captured at reception.
- **Vitals Snapshot**: View real-time vitals (BP, Heart Rate, Temperature, Weight, Height, and Blood Group).

### Gynaecology & Obstetrics (Specialist Intake)
For female patients, a specialized **Women's Health Panel** appears if specialist data was captured at reception (e.g., Gravida/Para status, EDD, LMP, or pregnancy complications). Expand this panel to review high-signal obstetric context.

### Clinical Documentation
- **Doctor Intake Note**: Document your examination findings and clinical reasoning in the provided text area.
- **Save Note**: Ensure you click "Save Note" to persist your findings to the patient's electronic medical record (EMR).

## 3. Prescriptions & Orders
### Medicines Tab
Use this tab to issue prescriptions that link directly to the Pharmacy module.
1.  **Search Formulary**: Search for medicines by brand or generic name.
2.  **Dosing**: Specify the dosage, frequency (e.g., TDS, BD), and duration.
3.  **Finalize**: Submit the order to make it available for dispensing at the pharmacy.

### Labs Tab
Order diagnostic tests for the patient.
1.  **Select Tests**: Choose required investigations from the lab catalogue.
2.  **Monitor Status**: Check if previous orders have been completed or are pending.

## 4. History & Analytics
- **History Tab**: Access the full chronological record of the patient's past visits, notes, and prescriptions.
- **Dashboard Tab**: Review your daily statistics, patient load, and clinical summaries.

## 5. Finalizing the Encounter
Once the consultation is finished:
- **Mark As Seen**: Click this to finalize the session. This moves the patient to the "Seen" status and unlocks the billing/dispensing flow for other departments.
- **Reopen/Cancel**: If a mistake was made or the patient left without consultation, use the "Reopen" or "Cancel" buttons in the workspace header.

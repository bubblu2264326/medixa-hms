# Market Direction — Where We Take This

> Updated 2026-06-20 with research findings. Both research engines independently converged on the same direction below — strong corroboration.

## The sharpened thesis
**Multan-first, pharmacy-first, offline-resilient.** Win South Punjab with local presence + support that the big-city cloud incumbents don't offer, lead with the ROI-obvious pharmacy module on a hybrid (offline-capable) architecture, then expand to the full clinic/hospital suite.

## Architecture decision (now evidence-backed): **Hybrid — local-first + cloud sync**
Not pure cloud, not pure local. This is both the **right fit** for unreliable internet/electricity + data-trust anxiety, AND the **clearest market gap** — mass-market local SaaS players (InstaCare et al.) are cloud-only and do not solve offline.

| | Pure Cloud SaaS | Pure Local | **Hybrid (recommended)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Works in net/power outage | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Data safety/backup | ✅ off-site | ❌ DIY risk | ✅ local + encrypted cloud |
| Owner data-control trust | 🟡 | ✅ | ✅ |
| Cost / maintenance to us | ✅ / ✅ | ❌ / ❌ | 🟡 / 🟡 |

→ This is a **product/architecture requirement**, not just positioning. Feeds the upcoming audits.

## Path: wedge-and-expand (geography + segment)
1. **Beachhead (0–6 mo):** Multan — small clinics + standalone pharmacy/lab. On-ground onboarding/support. Fast ROI wins + references.
2. **Expand cities (6–12 mo):** Muzaffargarh, Layyah, Bhakkar, Mianwali.
3. **Expand segment (12–18 mo):** integrated suite to mid-size private hospitals.
4. **Upmarket (18 mo+):** diagnostic / multi-specialty chains; multi-branch + integrations.

## Product implications surfaced by research (must-haves to compete)
- **Offline / local-first + sync** (architecture).
- **FBR POS / digital invoicing** integration (billing + pharmacy).
- **DRAP-aware pharmacy**: batch/expiry, FEFO, 3-year retention, controlled-substance logging.
- **Urdu / Roman-Urdu UI** as first-class.
- **WhatsApp** appointments/reminders (not just SMS).
- **PHC-MSDS record-keeping readiness** (sales lever to licensed clinics).
- **Auth/security hardening + audit logging + consent** (PECA today, PDP law coming).

## Bets to validate with customers (not assume)
- Is pharmacy/billing ROI the sharpest entry pain in South Punjab? (discovery)
- Subscription vs one-time license expectation locally? `[RESEARCH NEEDED]`
- Will offline resilience actually win deals vs cheaper cloud-only? (pilot)

## Triggers that change the plan
- **PDP Act passes w/ data-localization** → in-country hosting becomes mandatory; make it default now.
- **PHC mandates digital record-keeping** → demand spikes; lead with compliance.
- **A funded incumbent opens South Punjab offices** → verticalize (pharmacy chains, labs) + lean harder on offline.

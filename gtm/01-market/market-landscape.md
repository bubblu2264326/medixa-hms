# Market Landscape — Healthcare IT, Pakistan / South Punjab

> Updated 2026-06-20 with sourced research. Remaining unknowns still flagged `[RESEARCH NEEDED]`. Sources in [../05-research/research-log.md](../05-research/research-log.md).

## Why this market
- Large private healthcare sector; many small clinics, pharmacies, labs, and private hospitals.
- **Cloud/web is now the default** for new clinic software both globally and in Pakistan — KLAS-cited data puts cloud at ~80% of new EHR purchases (secondary source — verify). Pakistani clinic vendors ship web-predominantly.
- But **desktop/offline persists** in pharmacies and low-connectivity towns due to power/internet gaps.
- Incumbents cluster in **Karachi / Lahore / Islamabad** → small-city South Punjab is under-served.

## Target geography: South Punjab
Beachhead cities: **Multan** (lead), then Muzaffargarh, Layyah, Bhakkar, Mianwali. The decisive structural realities here:
- **Unreliable internet + electricity** → offline tolerance matters.
- **Urdu / Roman-Urdu** is the working language.
- **Price sensitivity** is high; paper + point-tools are the status quo.
- **WhatsApp** is the dominant comms channel — Pakistan had ~52M WhatsApp users (World Population Review, 2024), with some trackers estimating 95–105M; far higher engagement than SMS.

## Segments
| Segment | Buying behavior | Our wedge |
|---|---|---|
| Single-doctor / small clinics | Owner = buyer, fast, price-led | Clinic + queue + billing, transparent price |
| Pharmacies / labs | Want stock/billing ROI | Pharmacy-POS, FBR-ready, offline |
| Mid-size private hospitals | Committee, longer cycle | Integrated suite |
| Diagnostic / multi-specialty chains | Central IT, integrations | Platform / multi-branch |

## Demand drivers
- Revenue leakage (untracked stock, billing gaps).
- **FBR POS** invoicing pressure on retail/pharmacy.
- **PHC licensing + MSDS** record-keeping needs (Punjab).
- Smartphone + WhatsApp ubiquity lowering adoption barrier.

## Barriers / risks
- Trust + switching cost (data migration, downtime fear).
- Connectivity/power gaps → must be offline-tolerant.
- Low willingness to pay → cheap to buy + run.
- No enacted data-protection law yet, but one is coming (see regulatory checklist) → build for it now.

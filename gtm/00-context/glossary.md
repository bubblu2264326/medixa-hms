# Glossary — HMS + GTM terms

## Product / clinical
- **HMS** — Hospital Management System: software running clinical + operational workflows of a healthcare provider.
- **EMR / EHR** — Electronic Medical / Health Record. EMR = single-provider clinical record; EHR = broader, shareable across providers.
- **OPD** — Out-Patient Department (walk-in/consultation visits). Our `visit_type` is `OPD` or `Emergency`.
- **POS** — Point of Sale (here, pharmacy dispensing/checkout).
- **RBAC** — Role-Based Access Control.
- **Visit lifecycle** — the status path a patient visit moves through (waiting → seen_by_doctor → … → completed).
- **OTC** — Over-the-counter pharmacy sale (no linked visit).

## GTM
- **GTM** — Go-To-Market: how a product reaches and wins customers.
- **ICP** — Ideal Customer Profile: the customer type we're built to serve best.
- **Wedge** — the narrow, fast-to-sell entry point used to land accounts before expanding.
- **ACV** — Annual Contract Value.
- **CAC** — Customer Acquisition Cost.
- **LTV** — Lifetime Value of a customer.
- **MRR / ARR** — Monthly / Annual Recurring Revenue.
- **Pipeline** — set of deals in progress, organized by stage.
- **Beachhead** — the first segment/market you dominate before expanding.
- **Land-and-expand** — sell a small initial footprint, grow account over time.

# ICP & Segments

## Primary ICP (beachhead)

**Small private clinic or standalone pharmacy/lab in an urban/peri-urban area of Pakistan/South Asia**, owner-operated, 1–10 staff, currently on paper + a billing/POS tool, feeling revenue leakage and admin chaos, with the owner as the decision-maker (fast, single-buyer sale).

**Qualifying signals (good fit):**
- Owner-operated or small management team (short decision chain).
- Already losing money/time to manual stock + billing.
- Has basic smartphones/PC + internet.
- 1–3 locations.

**Disqualifiers (for now):**
- Large hospital needing deep insurance/claims + legacy integrations.
- No connectivity / unwilling to change workflow.
- Expecting free/open-source self-hosting.

## Segment sequencing (wedge-and-expand)

| # | Segment | Why this order | Lead module | Sales motion | ACV |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Small clinics / single-doctor | Fastest decision, huge volume | Clinic + queue + billing | Direct, owner-led | Low |
| 2 | Pharmacies / labs | Clear money ROI, big pool | Pharmacy-POS / lab | Direct + supplier partners | Low–med |
| 3 | Mid-size private hospitals | Full-suite value, references | Integrated suite | Consultative, committee | Med–high |
| 4 | Diagnostic / multi-specialty chains | High value, sticky | Platform + multi-branch | Strategic, top-down | High |

## Personas

- **The Owner (economic buyer)** — cares about revenue, cost, control. Pitch: ROI, leakage stopped, one system.
- **The Doctor (user/influencer)** — cares about speed, patient history. Pitch: prescribe fast, full history.
- **The Front-desk/Cashier (daily user)** — cares about ease, fewer errors. Pitch: simple screens, real-time queue.
- **The Pharmacist (daily user + ROI)** — cares about stock accuracy. Pitch: never lose stock or a sale.
- **Hospital Admin / IT (gatekeeper, segments 3–4)** — cares about reliability, integrations, security. Pitch: one source of truth, audit logs, RBAC.

# Pricing Strategy (Framework)

> ⚠️ **No prices are set here.** Pricing must be derived from real cost + willingness-to-pay + competitor research. All amounts are `[RESEARCH NEEDED]`. This doc defines the *approach*, not the numbers.

## Pricing principles
1. **Affordable-by-design** — must clear the SMB emerging-market budget bar.
2. **ROI-anchored** — price as a fraction of the leakage/time we save (esp. pharmacy/billing).
3. **Land small, expand** — low-friction entry price, grow by modules + locations + seats.
4. **Predictable** — favor simple recurring SaaS over opaque quotes (validate vs. local norms).

## Model options to test

| Model | Fit | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| Per-location monthly SaaS | Clinics, pharmacies | Simple, recurring | Caps value capture for big sites |
| Per-seat / per-doctor | Clinics, hospitals | Scales with usage | Can deter adding users |
| Module-based (wedge → suite) | All | Matches land-and-expand | More SKUs to manage |
| One-time license + AMC | If market expects it `[RESEARCH NEEDED]` | Matches legacy expectation | Hurts recurring revenue |
| Freemium / free pilot | Beachhead acquisition | Lowers trial friction | Conversion risk |

**Likely starting structure (hypothesis to validate):** module-based + per-location monthly SaaS, with a free/low-cost pilot to land.

## Packaging hypothesis (wedge-and-expand)
- **Starter (wedge):** clinic OR pharmacy/lab module, single location.
- **Suite:** full integrated system (clinical + pharmacy + lab + billing + HR).
- **Multi-location / chain:** suite + central management + integrations (segment 4).

## Market price benchmarks (researched 2026-06-20 — indicative, verify)
- Lowest published local anchor: **~PKR 1,500/month** (e-mareez clinic; Websol pharmacy POS) — these are the rare transparent-price players.
- Most incumbents (InstaCare, Healthwire, Oscar, Moneypex, Lifeline) are **custom-quote only** — opacity is itself an opening: transparent PKR pricing builds trust with small owners.
- Indicative one-time desktop pricing seen (low-authority source): Easy One Soft ~PKR 15,000 one-time. Treat as rough.
- **Implication:** anchor a single-doctor / single-pharmacy tier near the PKR 1,500/mo band, with modular add-ons (lab/HR/multi-branch). Undercut hidden-quote incumbents on transparency, not just price.

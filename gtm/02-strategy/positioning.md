# Positioning

## Positioning statement (draft)

> For **small and mid-size healthcare providers in Pakistan/South Asia** who are **running on paper and disconnected tools**, **[Product]** is an **all-in-one Hospital Management System** that **runs the entire patient journey and back office on one integrated platform** — unlike **enterprise EHRs (too costly) and single-module point tools (don't connect)** — because it is **built for emerging-market budgets with pharmacy, billing, and stock integrity enforced at the database level**.

## Category
We play in **Hospital/Clinic Management Software (HMS)**, anchored as the **integrated, affordable** option. We do **not** position as an enterprise EHR.

## Value proposition by audience

| Audience | Their pain | Our promise |
|---|---|---|
| Clinic owner | Chaos, revenue leakage, no visibility | "Run your whole clinic from one screen — and stop losing money." |
| Pharmacy/lab owner | Stock leakage, manual billing | "Never lose track of stock or a sale again." |
| Hospital admin | Many systems, no single source of truth | "One system for clinical, pharmacy, billing, and staff." |
| Doctor | Scattered patient info | "Full patient history, prescribe in seconds." |

## Messaging pillars (proof must come from real features — see product-capabilities)
1. **All-in-one** — one system replaces 3–4 tools.
2. **Money-tight** — stock + billing integrity protects revenue (DB-enforced).
3. **Built for here** — priced and designed for regional SMB providers.
4. **Fast to run** — role-based screens, real-time queue, low training overhead.

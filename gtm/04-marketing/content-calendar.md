# Content Calendar (Template)

> Keep it small and real in the beachhead. Quality + proof over volume. Every claim traces to product-capabilities or a real pilot result.

## Content pillars
1. **Proof** — case studies, ROI stories (real pilots only).
2. **Education** — "how to stop stock leakage", "running a paperless clinic".
3. **Product** — demo videos, feature walk-throughs per segment.
4. **Trust** — security/RBAC/audit explainer (honest about maturity).

## Asset backlog
- Product one-pager (per segment).
- Demo videos (clinic, pharmacy, hospital).
- Case study template (fill from real pilots).
- Credibility website + about.
- Pitch deck (if raising — see intent).

## Rules
- No fabricated metrics or testimonials.
- Reuse the messaging-framework language for consistency.
- Track which content actually drives demos/pilots.

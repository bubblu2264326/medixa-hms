# Channels Plan

## Principle
In the beachhead, **direct + referral + partnerships** beat broad paid marketing. Healthcare SMB buying is trust-driven and network-based.

## Channel priorities (beachhead)

| Priority | Channel | Why | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Direct outreach (WhatsApp/calls/visits) | Owner-led sale, fast | Targeted lists by city/segment |
| 2 | Referrals | Trust travels in provider networks | Systematic ask post-pilot |
| 3 | Partnerships | Distribution leverage | Pharmacy/medical suppliers, medical equipment vendors, associations |
| 4 | Content (proof) | Builds credibility | Case studies, how-to, demo videos |
| 5 | Local events / associations | Concentrated buyers | Medical/pharmacy associations, expos |
| 6 | Paid (later) | Scale after PMF | Search/social once message + economics proven |

## Partnership ideas to explore
- Pharmaceutical distributors / medical suppliers (they touch every pharmacy).
- Medical equipment / hardware vendors (printers, devices — ties to our hardware module).
- Medical & pharmacy associations.
- Clinic-setup consultants / accountants serving providers.

## Content engine (lightweight)
- 1 flagship case study per reference customer (ROI-led, real numbers only).
- Short demo videos per segment workflow.
- "How [provider] stopped losing money on stock" style stories (real).
- A simple credibility website + one-pager.

## Channel metrics
- Leads + cost per channel · pilot rate · close rate by source · referral rate. Track which channel produces customers, double down.

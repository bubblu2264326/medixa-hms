# Messaging Framework

## Core message (one line)
**"Run your entire clinic, pharmacy, and hospital on one system — built for [region], priced for you."**

## Tagline options (pick + test — do not invent proof)
- "One system. Your whole practice."
- "Stop losing money to paper."
- "The HMS built for clinics like yours."

## Message hierarchy

**Level 1 — Headline:** All-in-one HMS for SMB providers in [region].

**Level 2 — Pillars:**
1. **All-in-one** — replaces 3–4 disconnected tools.
2. **Protects revenue** — stock + billing integrity enforced automatically.
3. **Built for here** — regional fit and price.
4. **Easy to run** — role screens, real-time queue, low training.

**Level 3 — Proof (must be true; pull from product-capabilities):**
- Integrated single-database suite (real).
- Trigger-enforced inventory/POS (real).
- 7 role workspaces shipped (real).
- Pilot ROI / customer results — `[RESEARCH NEEDED: from pilots]`.

## Message by segment
- **Clinics:** "Your whole clinic on one screen."
- **Pharmacies/labs:** "Never lose stock or a sale."
- **Hospitals:** "One source of truth across every department."

## Voice & tone
- Direct, concrete, outcome-led. Numerals for numbers.
- Avoid hype/vague claims ("transform", "revolutionize", "world-class").
- Lead with the customer's money/time, not our tech.

## Banned (integrity)
- No invented stats, customer counts, or ROI figures.
- No claims for unbuilt features (telemedicine, insurance, certifications).

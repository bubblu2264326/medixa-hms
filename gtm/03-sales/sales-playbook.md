# Sales Playbook

## Motion
- **Beachhead (segments 1–2):** founder-led, high-touch direct sales + referrals. Owner is the buyer → short cycle.
- **Hospitals (segment 3):** consultative, multi-stakeholder, demo + pilot.
- **Chains (segment 4):** strategic, top-down, longer.

## The pitch (30-second version)
"You're running your clinic/pharmacy on paper and separate tools, and it's costing you money — untracked stock, missed billing, no single view of a patient. [Product] runs your whole operation on one system: front desk, doctor, pharmacy, lab, billing, and staff — built for clinics like yours and priced for here. Want a 10-minute demo on your own workflow?"

## Discovery questions (qualify + find pain)
1. How do you track patients, prescriptions, and bills today?
2. How do you manage pharmacy/lab stock and dispensing?
3. Where do you think you lose money or time?
4. How many locations / doctors / staff?
5. Who decides on tools like this? (find the buyer)
6. What's stopped you from adopting software before? (price/trust/effort/connectivity)
7. What does internet/hardware look like at your site?

## Qualification (lightweight BANT)
- **Budget:** can/will pay recurring? `[confirm against pricing]`
- **Authority:** talking to the owner/decision-maker?
- **Need:** real, felt pain (leakage, chaos)?
- **Timing:** willing to pilot now?

## Demo principles
- Demo **their** workflow (clinic vs pharmacy vs hospital), not a feature tour.
- Lead with the **money** module (pharmacy/billing) for ROI segments.
- Show real-time queue + one-screen patient journey for clinics.
- **Be honest about gaps** (auth hardening, roadmap items) — see capabilities doc.

## Pilot → close
1. Free/low-cost time-boxed pilot at one location.
2. Define success upfront (e.g. stock accuracy, billing captured, time saved).
3. Capture ROI → convert to paid → ask for referral + case study.

## Do-not (org guardrails)
- Don't quote/send pricing without owner sign-off.
- Don't promise unbuilt features.
- Don't expose other customers' data; handle patient data per policy.

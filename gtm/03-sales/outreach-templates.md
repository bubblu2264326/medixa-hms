# Outreach Templates

> Drafts — personalize per provider. Keep claims true. **Do not include pricing** without owner sign-off (org guardrail). Replace `[...]` before sending. Nothing here should be sent automatically.

## Cold — clinic owner (WhatsApp/SMS, short)
> Hi Dr. [Name] — I built an all-in-one system for clinics like [Clinic]: front desk, prescriptions, pharmacy, billing, and reports in one place. Could I show you a 10-min demo on how it'd run *your* clinic? No cost to look.

## Cold — pharmacy/lab owner (ROI angle)
> Hi [Name] — most pharmacies lose money to untracked stock and missed billing. I have a system that keeps stock and sales exact automatically. Open to a quick demo? I can run it on your actual workflow.

## Cold — hospital admin (email)
> **Subject:** One system for [Hospital]'s clinical + pharmacy + billing + staff
>
> Hi [Name], hospitals like [Hospital] often run several disconnected tools. [Product] puts the patient journey (reception → doctor → lab → pharmacy → billing) and the back office (inventory, attendance, staff) on one integrated platform — built and priced for providers in [region].
> Worth a 20-minute demo? I can tailor it to your departments.
> — [You]

## Referral ask (after a happy pilot)
> Glad [outcome] is working for you. Do you know 1–2 other [clinics/pharmacies] who'd benefit? A quick intro helps a lot — happy to give them the same pilot.

## Follow-up (no reply)
> Hi [Name], following up — still happy to show a quick demo whenever suits. Even if now's not the time, I can share a 2-min overview so you have it.

## Notes
- Lead with **their** pain/segment, not features.
- One clear CTA: a short demo.
- Track every send + reply in the pipeline; log loss reasons.

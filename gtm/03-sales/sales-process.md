# Sales Process & Pipeline

## Pipeline stages

| # | Stage | Entry criteria | Exit criteria | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Lead | Identified provider that fits ICP | Contact made | Sales |
| 1 | Discovery | Reached decision-maker | Pain + fit qualified (BANT) | Sales |
| 2 | Demo | Qualified | Demoed on their workflow; interest confirmed | Sales |
| 3 | Pilot | Agreed to pilot | Pilot live at 1 location | Sales + Onboarding |
| 4 | Validate | Pilot running | Success metric hit (ROI shown) | Onboarding |
| 5 | Close | ROI proven | Signed / paying | Sales (+ owner sign-off on price) |
| 6 | Expand | Live + happy | More modules / locations / referral | Account |

## Stage hygiene
- One clear **next step + date** on every active deal.
- Track **why** deals stall/lose (price? trust? effort? connectivity?) → feeds product + pricing.
- A deal with no next step in 14 days → re-qualify or drop.

## Metrics to track (set targets after first cohort)
- Lead → Discovery → Demo → Pilot → Close conversion per stage.
- Sales cycle length by segment.
- Pilot → paid conversion %.
- CAC and payback (once spend is real).
- Loss reasons (categorized).

## Tooling
- CRM/pipeline tracker — `[decide tool]`. Until then, a simple sheet with the stages above.
- Capture every deal's loss/win reason — it's the most valuable GTM data early on.

## Handoffs
- **Sales → Onboarding** at Pilot: site details, hardware, data to migrate, success metric.
- **Onboarding → Account** at Expand: usage health, expansion openings.
